Why “The Sunday Farm
Children’s Home”?

“Sunday” represents a fresh start, a day of rest, worship, and hope. “Farm” represents growth, cultivation, and love taking root. Together, they reflect our mission to raise healthy, whole children who know Jesus and bring healing to the world.
