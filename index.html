Why “The Sunday Farm
Children’s Home”?

“Sunday” represents a fresh start, a day of rest, worship, and hope. “Farm” represents growth, cultivation, and love taking root. Together, they reflect our mission to raise healthy, whole children who expereince the love of Jesus through caring adults.
