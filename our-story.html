Our Story

The Beginning

The Sunday Farm Children’s Home was born out of heartbreak and a journey of healing. Our Founder, Ales, experienced sexual molestation at just seven years old. That one incident sent her into years of confusion, broken identity, depression, addiction, and deep pain. For much of her life, she lived as a reflection of that trauma rather than the person God created her to be.

In her twenties, Ales found herself caught in repeating cycles of the same trauma until 2020, when her father became seriously ill. Desperate for a miracle, she turned to the only One known for healing—Jesus. She prayed for her father’s restoration, and within months, God answered. From that moment, she recommitted her life fully to Jesus, choosing to walk in His love and be led by the Holy Spirit.

Five years later, Ales stands healed and whole—freed from pain she once believed would define her forever caused by molestation and assault. During her healing journey, she came across an article about an eleven-year-old who assaulted an eight-year-old. That story broke her heart and ignited a holy fire within her. She vowed to join the Lord in freeing children from the grip of assault—just as He had freed her.

We are raising healthy, whole children whose love for Jesus and inner restoration bring healing to the world around them.

From that vow, The Sunday Farm Children’s Home was born: a Christ-centered safe haven where children come in and are changed forever. Every child who comes to us carries their own story, questions, and need for a fresh start. “Sunday” represents that new beginning; “Farm” represents the steady growth, cultivation, and care that take place in our homes—not fast or flashy, but deep and real.

Here, love is planted and healing takes root. We are raising healthy, whole children whose love for Jesus and inner restoration bring healing to the world around them. We don’t force faith, we simply invite children into our homes and into a relationship with the One who saved our founder, Ales, and so many of our team.

Our vision is to raise children who are led by the Holy Spirit and grow to be disciples of Jesus—carrying transformative love to every corner of the world. We also walk alongside parents and caregivers who are navigating the pain of discovering their child has been harmed. No parent should walk that road alone.

Raising a child in the way they should go or walking alongside a family who’s stewarding a healing child isn’t a job done by one person. It takes a community: a family. Join our family by getting involved today. A healed & whole child is on the other side of your YES.
