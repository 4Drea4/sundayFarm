FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our services and programs.

1 How do I place a child in your care?

Thank you for trusting The Sunday Farm Children’s Home with a child’s care. Whether you’re a parent, guardian, or case representative, your partnership begins the journey of healing and hope. Please complete the form below, and our team will contact you soon. You can also email us at info@sundayfarm.org.

Start Intake Process

2 What ages do you serve?

We primarily serve children up to age 14. Older children may be accommodated case-by-case. Caregiver support is available for all ages.

3 What services do you provide?

Residential care (respite, emergency, short-term, long-term), therapeutic and educational support, caregiver programs, reunification services, and community engagement.

4 Is there a cost for your services?

We are a completely free service for families and caregivers—whether state-appointed or familial—who are caring for children who have experienced trauma or neglect. Our work is entirely donor-funded, and we never require payment for our services. However, we gratefully accept donations of any kind.

We understand that needs arise and resources aren’t always within reach. If you’d like help finding support or requesting specific items, please contact us.
